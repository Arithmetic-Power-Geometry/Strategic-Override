## MSOD01 — Regional Market Entry

**Domain:** Market entry  
**Context:** A mid-sized manufacturing firm

**Common evidence (same factual content in both representation conditions):**

- Demand in the target region is forecast to grow by 9% annually for the next three years.
- Entry requires an initial investment of ₹80 crore.
- Expected operating margin before financing is 11%.
- Two established competitors together hold about 58% of the market.
- The firm's existing distribution network can cover roughly 60% of the target region.

**High-QC candidate organising question:** Does the expected demand and operating margin justify the ₹80 crore entry cost after accounting for competitive pressure and distribution coverage?

**Human-only evidence, present condition:** A formally approved state incentive will reimburse 35% of qualifying entry investment, and the approval was not available to the AI system.

**Absent-condition text:** No additional verified information affecting the entry economics is available beyond the common evidence.

**Declared criterion without human-only evidence:** reject

**Declared criterion with human-only evidence:** approve

## MSOD02 — Acquisition of a Smaller Competitor

**Domain:** Mergers and acquisitions  
**Context:** A national business-services company

**Common evidence (same factual content in both representation conditions):**

- The target has grown revenue by 14% annually over the last two years.
- The proposed purchase price is 11.5 times EBITDA.
- Customer concentration is high: the largest three customers contribute 46% of target revenue.
- Estimated annual cost synergies are ₹18 crore.
- Integration is expected to take 18 months.

**High-QC candidate organising question:** Do expected growth and ₹18 crore annual synergies compensate for the acquisition multiple, customer concentration, and 18-month integration burden?

**Human-only evidence, present condition:** A signed five-year renewal from the target's largest customer reduces projected concentration risk materially; this contract was not available to the AI system.

**Absent-condition text:** No additional verified contract or customer-retention information is available beyond the common evidence.

**Declared criterion without human-only evidence:** reject

**Declared criterion with human-only evidence:** approve

## MSOD03 — Launch of a Premium Product Line

**Absent-condition text:** No additional verified distribution commitment is available beyond the common evidence.

## MSOD04 — Enterprise AI Automation Platform

**Domain:** AI investment  
**Context:** A diversified services firm

**Common evidence (same factual content in both representation conditions):**

- The proposed AI platform costs ₹28 crore over three years.
- A pilot reduced average processing time by 23%.
- The pilot covered only 8% of the firm's transaction volume.
- Estimated annual labour and error-cost savings are ₹9 crore.
- The platform requires integration with four legacy systems.

**High-QC candidate organising question:** Do validated pilot gains and projected savings justify the three-year AI investment once limited pilot coverage and legacy-system integration are considered?

**Human-only evidence, present condition:** A completed technical audit confirms that three of the four legacy integrations can use existing certified interfaces, reducing implementation cost by ₹8 crore; this audit was not available to the AI system.

**Absent-condition text:** No additional verified integration evidence is available beyond the common evidence.

**Declared criterion without human-only evidence:** reject

**Declared criterion with human-only evidence:** approve

## MSOD05 — Dual-Sourcing Critical Components

**Domain:** Supply-chain strategy  
**Context:** An electronics manufacturer

**Common evidence (same factual content in both representation conditions):**

- The current sole supplier is 7% cheaper than the proposed secondary supplier.
- The current supplier has delivered on time in 94% of orders.
- A production stoppage costs approximately ₹1.6 crore per day.
- The component has an average replenishment lead time of 38 days.
- Qualifying the second supplier requires a one-time cost of ₹4.5 crore.

**High-QC candidate organising question:** Does the resilience value of dual sourcing justify its price premium and qualification cost given disruption cost, present reliability, and replenishment lead time?

**Human-only evidence, present condition:** A verified regulatory filing shows the current supplier's primary plant will undergo a mandatory six-week shutdown within the next year; this information was not available to the AI system.

**Absent-condition text:** No additional verified disruption information is available beyond the common evidence.

**Declared criterion without human-only evidence:** reject

**Declared criterion with human-only evidence:** approve

## MSOD06 — New Production Capacity

**Context:** A specialty-chemicals producer

**Common evidence (same factual content in both representation conditions):**

**Absent-condition text:** No additional verified offtake commitment is available beyond the common evidence.

## MSOD08 — Premium Pricing Increase

**Domain:** Pricing strategy  
**Context:** A subscription-based software provider

**Common evidence (same factual content in both representation conditions):**
