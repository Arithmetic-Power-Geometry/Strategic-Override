# Preregistration Template

## Primary question
When does a decision maker appropriately override an AI recommendation in a strategic decision?

## Confirmatory factors
1. AI correctness: correct vs incorrect.
2. Human-only information: absent vs present.
3. Candidate representation: low-QC candidate vs high-QC candidate.

## Primary outcomes
1. Decision accuracy.
2. Override.
3. Justified override.
4. Unjustified override.

## Secondary outcomes
Confidence revision, decision revision, response time, and reason coding.

## Planned tests
- H1: Human-only decision-relevant information increases justified override when the AI recommendation is incorrect.
- H2: After successful manipulation pretesting, the structured representation condition improves discrimination between appropriate reliance and appropriate override.
- H3: The interaction between representation and human-only information predicts justified override.
- H4: Override Selectivity Index is higher when decision-relevant exclusive information is available.
- H5: Information-removal contrasts alter final decisions when the removed information is criterion-relevant.

## Required decisions before recruitment
- exact sample-size calculation;
- alpha and multiplicity approach;
- model family;
- participant/case random effects or clustering strategy;
- exclusion rules;
- missing-data rule;
- manipulation-check success criterion;
- whether decision reasons are confirmatory or exploratory.

Do not register a confirmatory claim that formal DPT Question Compression or full Experience Architecture has been measured unless an independent measurement model has been validated.
